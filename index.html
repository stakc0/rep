02 / THE IDEA

Good traders leave
receipts.

Crypto has an endless supply of wallet trackers, leaderboards, and “alpha” feeds. It has almost nowhere to answer the question that matters most:

“What kind of person am I following?”

REP turns wallet history into a living public profile. People can review a trader, attach evidence, recognize good behavior, and warn the next person before they send funds or copy a position.
