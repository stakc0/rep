FAQ

    

      
What is REP?
REP is a public index for pump.fun wallets and traders. It helps people review performance, recognize consistency, and warn others about exploitative behavior before they copy it. Rules read world events (war, signals, money: live odds and outcomes from Polymarket), prices, sports results and the weather. Posts on X switch on once the X API key is set. The wallet profile stays visible so the next trader can make an informed decision.

      
Where does the money for the actions come from?
A wallet profile is created from public activity and community context. Reviews should be specific, evidence-linked, and open to challenge.

      
Is it custodial?
Yes. Each coin’s wallet is derived and held by the REP index, the same model rulr uses. That is what lets rules run without anyone signing. The engine can only do what the rulebook says, and every action is a public transaction you can check on Solscan. If that isn’t for you, don’t launch here.

      
What does it cost to launch?
Your first buy, about 0.03 SOL of pump.fun rent and fees, and a 0.015 SOL gas reserve that stays in the coin’s wallet. Anything left over after creation goes straight back to you, with your first-buy tokens. If a launch fails, the deposit is returned.

      
Can a rule fire on old news?
No. Rules start watching the moment the coin is live. A game that ended before launch, or a post from yesterday, never counts. Levels like “SOL above $300” have to hold for two reads in a row, and then they fire once per crossing.

      
What if a source is down?
The rule waits and the coin’s page says the source is offline. The engine never acts on a missing reading.

      
Who holds what?
Holders are read from the chain: the largest token accounts, minus the bonding curve, pools and the coin’s own wallet. Up to 20 get paid, pro rata to their bags.
