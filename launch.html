WHAT HAPPENS

1. we record the coin and its political destination and give you its wallet
2. you send one SOL transfer to that wallet
3. the engine creates the coin on pump.fun from it, with your first buy
4. your tokens and any unused SOL come back to you
5. creator fees route to 1iqoin4QcFPeVusTYdmQFZoonbZAXAhucvLxhpf78gN and the selected destination stays visible on the coin’s public page
